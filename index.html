Website pricing and process: common questions

How much does a website cost?

A static website is $100 and a dynamic website is $200, both one-time per site. Custom projects such as redesigns and custom software are priced after a quick call at +1 646 965 6598.

Do you build websites for businesses in Miami and across Florida?

Yes. I work with businesses in Miami and across Florida, by phone and online. I speak English and Spanish. Call +1 646 965 6598 to get started.

What is the difference between the static and the dynamic website?

The static website is a simple site with a payment gateway connected, your content and your branding. The dynamic website is data-driven and includes SEO and data analytics. Both are mobile-friendly.

Can I accept online payments on the $100 website?

Yes, the static website includes a connected payment gateway. Gateway fees are charged by the payment provider.

What does "SEO and data analytics included" mean?

Dynamic websites include SEO and data analytics, so you can see who visits and what they do. Call to discuss the details of your project.

How does the process work?

We start with a market analysis of your business and 10 leading competitors and industry references, then build a first draft and deliver a working MVP. We add details and tools until your website reflects your company's value proposition, and then follow up with ongoing improvements and SEO.
